### Usage rules

- Use the navy-on-light versions on white or light backgrounds and the navy version on dark ones. Never swap them.
- Keep clear space on every side equal to the height of the "P". No type, photos, or other logos inside it.
- Minimum sizes: wordmark 120 px wide, icon tile 32 px.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows or effects, or place the mark on a busy photo.
- Do not recolor the gold. It is the only accent color in the system.
- Do not re-type the wordmark in a font. It is drawn artwork.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | `#0A2240` | Primary. Backgrounds, headings, the P |
| Navy Deep | `#061627` | Gradient end, footers |
| Gold | `#C9A227` | Accent only. The L, rules, one highlighted word |
| Gold Light | `#E2C466` | Small reversed type on navy |
| Slate | `#5A6B80` | Secondary text and captions |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Reversed type on navy |

Gold on navy passes contrast at 18 pt and above. Do not set body copy in gold.

## Typography
